About Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank IFSC codes

Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank is a district central co-operative bank. It has 1 branch with an IFSC code in 1 state or union territory. Its IFSC codes begin with UTIB, the bank code of Axis Bank. Smaller banks often send and receive NEFT and RTGS payments through a larger bank (a sub-member arrangement), and their IFSC codes then carry that bank's four letters. An example is UTIB0SBLDB1 (Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank Imps, Mumbai).

Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank is a district central co-operative bank. A district central co-operative bank works in one district. It is the middle level of the co-operative credit system: village credit societies bank with it, and it banks with the state co-operative bank. It lends mostly for farming and to people in the rural parts of the district.

All its branches with an IFSC code are in Maharashtra.

Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank does not have a bank code of its own in these payment systems. It is a sub-member of Axis Bank: payments to its accounts travel through Axis Bank, which is why its IFSC codes begin with UTIB. For the sender nothing changes: use the code of the Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank branch exactly as it is given.

Deposit insurance: Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank is on the list of insured banks of DICGC, the deposit insurer owned by the Reserve Bank of India. Deposits there are insured up to ₹5 lakh for each depositor, counting savings, current, fixed and recurring deposits in the bank together, principal and interest.

What is the IFSC code of Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank?

Can I use one Buldana District Central Co-operative Bank IFSC code for another branch?

Use the code of the branch where the account is held. A payment with the right account number and another branch code of the same bank usually still arrives, because banks match the account number, but it can be delayed or returned, so always use the exact code.
