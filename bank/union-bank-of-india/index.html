About Union Bank of India IFSC codes

Union Bank of India is a public sector bank. It has 11,254 branches with an IFSC code in 35 states and union territories. Its IFSC codes begin with UBIN. An example is UBIN0530018 (Guwahati Main, Kamrup).

  • Most branches: Andhra Pradesh (2,824), Uttar Pradesh (1,304), Maharashtra (885), Karnataka (834), Tamil Nadu (622).
  • Places with the most branches: Guntur (293), East Godavari (216), Bangalore Urban (205), West Godavari (198), Krishna (179), Greater Bombay (159).
  • 2,104 of 11,254 branches have a MICR code (the 9-digit code printed on cheques).
  • NEFT: all but 6 branches. RTGS: all but 94 branches.
  • 11,254 branches are listed as UPI enabled.
  • 761 older codes of this bank are no longer in use: see the old codes.

Union Bank of India is a public sector bank. A public sector bank is a bank in which the Government of India holds most of the shares. It is regulated by the Reserve Bank of India like every other bank, and it usually has a wide branch network, including in small towns and villages, and handles many government payments and pension accounts.

Its branches are spread over 35 states and union territories; the most are in Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra.

Banks that became part of Union Bank of India: Andhra Bank (1 April 2020), Corporation Bank (1 April 2020). Their old IFSC codes are listed with the new ones on the pages linked here.

Deposit insurance: Union Bank of India is on the list of insured banks of DICGC, the deposit insurer owned by the Reserve Bank of India. Deposits there are insured up to ₹5 lakh for each depositor, counting savings, current, fixed and recurring deposits in the bank together, principal and interest.

Questions

What is the IFSC code of Union Bank of India?

Every branch of Union Bank of India has its own IFSC code. The codes begin with UBIN. For example, the code of Guwahati Main, Kamrup is UBIN0530018. Choose the state above to see the code of each branch.

How do I find the IFSC code of my Union Bank of India branch?

Choose your state on this page, then find your branch in the list or type its name in the search box. The code is also printed on your cheque book and passbook, and shown in the bank's app next to your account details.

Can I use one Union Bank of India IFSC code for another branch?

Use the code of the branch where the account is held. A payment with the right account number and another branch code of the same bank usually still arrives, because banks match the account number, but it can be delayed or returned, so always use the exact code.
