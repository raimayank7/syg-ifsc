Indian Overseas Bank is working as usual. These 387 codes were in an earlier list of the Reserve Bank and are not in the current one (data as of 1 September 2026). For 6 of them a current branch has the same name in the same place, and its code is shown. That is a match by name, not a statement from the bank, so confirm it before a payment. For the others, look for the branch in the current list of Indian Overseas Bank or ask the account holder.
