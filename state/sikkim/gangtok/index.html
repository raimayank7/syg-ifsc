How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Gangtok?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Gangtok has 18 branches of 10 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, SBPB, Gangtok is SBIN0013240; State Bank of India, Libing is SBIN0061622; State Bank of India, SME Branch Gangtok is SBIN0063793.

Which banks have the most branches in Gangtok?

State Bank of India (5), Yes Bank (3), North East Small Finance Bank (2), Reserve Bank of India (2), Punjab National Bank (1), UCO Bank (1).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Gangtok?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
