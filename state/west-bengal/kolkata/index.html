Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Kolkata?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Kolkata has 2,259 branches of 47 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Kolkata Main is SBIN0000001; State Bank of India, Alipore is SBIN0000004; State Bank of India, Ballygunge is SBIN0000018.

Which banks have the most branches in Kolkata?

State Bank of India (282), Punjab National Bank (251), ICICI Bank (176), HDFC Bank (157), Axis Bank (152), Bank of Baroda (127).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Kolkata?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
