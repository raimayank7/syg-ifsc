Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Nadia?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Nadia has 352 branches of 24 banks in our list. For example, Punjab National Bank, Krishnagar is PUNB0021520; Punjab National Bank, Nabadwip is PUNB0021620; Punjab National Bank, Santipur is PUNB0021720.

Which banks have the most branches in Nadia?

Punjab National Bank (68), State Bank of India (65), Bandhan Bank (45), Indian Bank (28), Nadia District Central Co-operative Bank (22), Axis Bank (20).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Nadia?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
