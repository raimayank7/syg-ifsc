Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Uttarkashi?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Uttarkashi has 31 branches of 11 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Uttarkashi is SBIN0001172; State Bank of India, Bhatwari is SBIN0002316; State Bank of India, Barkot is SBIN0003290.

Which banks have the most branches in Uttarkashi?

State Bank of India (19), Punjab National Bank (2), Union Bank of India (2), Nainital Bank (1), Axis Bank (1), Bandhan Bank (1).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Uttarkashi?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
