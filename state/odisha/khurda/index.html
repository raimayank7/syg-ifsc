Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Khurda?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Khurda has 579 branches of 34 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Bhubaneswar is SBIN0000041; State Bank of India, Khurda is SBIN0000116; State Bank of India, Jatni is SBIN0002071.

Which banks have the most branches in Khurda?

State Bank of India (112), Indian Overseas Bank (76), Union Bank of India (48), Canara Bank (43), Axis Bank (33), ICICI Bank (32).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Khurda?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
