How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Ganjam?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Ganjam has 338 branches of 30 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Aska is SBIN0000012; State Bank of India, Berhampur is SBIN0000033; State Bank of India, Bhanjanagar is SBIN0000038.

Which banks have the most branches in Ganjam?

State Bank of India (88), Union Bank of India (51), Canara Bank (24), Indian Overseas Bank (23), Indian Bank (20), HDFC Bank (15).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Ganjam?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
