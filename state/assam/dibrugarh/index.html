Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Dibrugarh?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Dibrugarh has 165 branches of 23 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Dibrugarh is SBIN0000071; State Bank of India, Namrup is SBIN0000223; State Bank of India, Dibrugarh University is SBIN0002051.

Which banks have the most branches in Dibrugarh?

State Bank of India (34), Punjab National Bank (27), Indian Bank (16), Canara Bank (12), HDFC Bank (9), North East Small Finance Bank (9).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Dibrugarh?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
