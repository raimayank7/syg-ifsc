How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Lucknow?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Lucknow has 1,298 branches of 48 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Lucknow Main BR. is SBIN0000125; State Bank of India, Swaroop Nagar Kanpur is SBIN0000217; State Bank of India, Charbagh is SBIN0001089.

Which banks have the most branches in Lucknow?

State Bank of India (169), Union Bank of India (111), Bank of Baroda (97), Punjab National Bank (97), HDFC Bank (92), Canara Bank (87).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Lucknow?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
