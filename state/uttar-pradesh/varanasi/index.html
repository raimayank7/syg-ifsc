Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Varanasi?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Varanasi has 594 branches of 40 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Varanasi is SBIN0000201; State Bank of India, Bhu,Varanasi is SBIN0000211; State Bank of India, Varanasi City is SBIN0001190.

Which banks have the most branches in Varanasi?

State Bank of India (99), Union Bank of India (79), Bank of Baroda (52), Punjab National Bank (42), Canara Bank (38), HDFC Bank (33).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Varanasi?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
