Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Allahabad?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Allahabad has 388 branches of 31 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Allahabad is SBIN0000006; State Bank of India, Naini is SBIN0000139; State Bank of India, Phulpur is SBIN0000155.

Which banks have the most branches in Allahabad?

State Bank of India (89), Bank of Baroda (66), Punjab National Bank (36), Union Bank of India (34), HDFC Bank (29), ICICI Bank (21).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Allahabad?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
