How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Agra?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Agra has 534 branches of 39 banks in our list. For example, Canara Bank, Jaithpur Kalan is CNRB0000115; Canara Bank, Kacherighat,Agra is CNRB0000168; Canara Bank, M G Road, Agra is CNRB0000192.

Which banks have the most branches in Agra?

Canara Bank (110), State Bank of India (91), Punjab National Bank (56), Union Bank of India (32), Bank of India (26), HDFC Bank (25).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Agra?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
