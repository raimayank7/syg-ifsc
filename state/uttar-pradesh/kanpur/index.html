Which banks have the most branches in Kanpur?

Punjab National Bank (41), State Bank of India (41), Bank of India (25), Union Bank of India (15), Axis Bank (14), Bank of Baroda (10).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Kanpur?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
