Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Dhar?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Dhar has 224 branches of 28 banks in our list. For example, Bank of India, Trimurti Nagar Dhar is BKID0008846; Bank of India, Gandhwani is BKID0008847; Bank of India, Dhar is BKID0009800.

Which banks have the most branches in Dhar?

Bank of India (80), State Bank of India (23), ICICI Bank (13), Bank of Baroda (11), HDFC Bank (11), Canara Bank (11).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Dhar?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
