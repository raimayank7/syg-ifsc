How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Bangalore Rural?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Bangalore Rural has 329 branches of 30 banks in our list. For example, Canara Bank, Bidadi is CNRB0000426; Canara Bank, Avathi is CNRB0000427; Canara Bank, Harohalli is CNRB0000433.

Which banks have the most branches in Bangalore Rural?

Canara Bank (72), State Bank of India (55), Union Bank of India (41), Bank of Baroda (21), Punjab National Bank (19), HDFC Bank (18).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Bangalore Rural?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
