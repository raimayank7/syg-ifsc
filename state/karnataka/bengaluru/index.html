Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Bengaluru?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Bengaluru has 293 branches of 39 banks in our list. For example, Canara Bank, Mis Wing HO is CNRB0000069; Canara Bank, Lead Ban And RRB Wing HO is CNRB0000070; Canara Bank, Dit Wing II Head Office is CNRB0000071.

Which banks have the most branches in Bengaluru?

Canara Bank (41), Bank of Baroda (30), Ujjivan Small Finance Bank (26), State Bank of India (23), Indusind Bank (19), City Union Bank (16).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Bengaluru?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
