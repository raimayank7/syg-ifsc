About bank branches in Chennai

  • PIN codes in the addresses: 600001, 600002, 600004, 600017, 600018, 600020, 600040, 600041 and others. Most begin with 600.
  • 2,148 of 2,548 branches have a MICR code, and most of them begin with 600 (the first three digits of a MICR code name the city).
  • NEFT: all but 1 branch. RTGS: all but 22 branches. IMPS: every branch.
  • A phone number is listed for 1,939 branches.

Which banks have the most branches in Chennai?

State Bank of India (272), HDFC Bank (206), ICICI Bank (180), Canara Bank (176), Axis Bank (153), Indian Overseas Bank (152).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Chennai?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
