Which banks have the most branches in Erode?

Canara Bank (80), Indian Overseas Bank (58), State Bank of India (54), HDFC Bank (24), Union Bank of India (22), Development Bank of Singapore (20).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Erode?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
