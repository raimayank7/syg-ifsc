Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Salem?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Salem has 488 branches of 40 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Attur is SBIN0000810; State Bank of India, Mettur Dam is SBIN0000877; State Bank of India, Salem is SBIN0000913.

Which banks have the most branches in Salem?

State Bank of India (62), Canara Bank (61), Indian Bank (48), Indian Overseas Bank (36), Union Bank of India (24), City Union Bank (24).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Salem?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
