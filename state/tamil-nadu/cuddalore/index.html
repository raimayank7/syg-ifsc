Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Cuddalore?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Cuddalore has 321 branches of 30 banks in our list. For example, Indian Bank, Annamalainagar is IDIB000A024; Indian Bank, Ayangudi is IDIB000A056; Indian Bank, Bhuvanagiri is IDIB000B060.

Which banks have the most branches in Cuddalore?

Indian Bank (40), Canara Bank (34), Indian Overseas Bank (31), ICICI Bank (26), State Bank of India (23), City Union Bank (17).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Cuddalore?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
