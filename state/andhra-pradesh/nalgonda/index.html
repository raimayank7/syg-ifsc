Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Nalgonda?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Nalgonda has 175 branches of 17 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Choutuppal is SBIN0002705; State Bank of India, Nalgonda is SBIN0006293; State Bank of India, Kodad is SBIN0006315.

Which banks have the most branches in Nalgonda?

State Bank of India (88), Canara Bank (20), Union Bank of India (13), Central Bank of India (13), Indian Overseas Bank (8), HDFC Bank (6).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Nalgonda?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
