Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Jammu?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Jammu has 441 branches of 29 banks in our list. For example, Jammu and Kashmir Bank, Allah Bishnah is JAKA0AALLAH; Jammu and Kashmir Bank, Agore is JAKA0AGHORE; Jammu and Kashmir Bank, Akhnoor is JAKA0AKHNOR.

Which banks have the most branches in Jammu?

Jammu and Kashmir Bank (116), State Bank of India (58), Punjab National Bank (45), HDFC Bank (39), ICICI Bank (34), Axis Bank (21).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Jammu?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
