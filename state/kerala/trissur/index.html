Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Trissur?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Trissur has 168 branches of 10 banks in our list. For example, South Indian Bank, Ammadam is SIBL0000003; South Indian Bank, Ayyanthole is SIBL0000007; South Indian Bank, Chalakudy is SIBL0000012.

Which banks have the most branches in Trissur?

South Indian Bank (71), State Bank of India (53), HDFC Bank (15), Union Bank of India (11), Canara Bank (9), Indian Overseas Bank (3).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Trissur?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
