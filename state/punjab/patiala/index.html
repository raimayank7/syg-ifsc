How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Patiala?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Patiala has 484 branches of 35 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Nabha is SBIN0001452; State Bank of India, Patiala is SBIN0001637; State Bank of India, Rajpura is SBIN0001712.

Which banks have the most branches in Patiala?

State Bank of India (107), Punjab National Bank (53), Punjab & Sind Bank (47), HDFC Bank (44), Axis Bank (36), ICICI Bank (23).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Patiala?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
