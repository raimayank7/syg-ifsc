Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Gaya?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Gaya has 193 branches of 29 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Gaya is SBIN0000079; State Bank of India, Gaya Bazar is SBIN0001222; State Bank of India, Bodh Gaya is SBIN0002739.

Which banks have the most branches in Gaya?

State Bank of India (44), Punjab National Bank (20), ICICI Bank (15), Bank of Baroda (12), Canara Bank (12), Union Bank of India (11).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Gaya?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
