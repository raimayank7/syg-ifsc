Questions

How do I find the IFSC code of a bank branch in Patna?

Choose the bank on this page, then find the branch by its name or address. Patna has 970 branches of 39 banks in our list. For example, State Bank of India, Barh is SBIN0000026; State Bank of India, Mokama is SBIN0000135; State Bank of India, Patna Main is SBIN0000152.

Which banks have the most branches in Patna?

State Bank of India (185), Canara Bank (89), Union Bank of India (76), Bank of Baroda (65), Punjab National Bank (58), Indian Bank (58).

Is the IFSC code the same for all branches in Patna?

No. Every branch of every bank has its own code. The first four letters name the bank and the last six name the branch, so two branches in the same town never share a code.
