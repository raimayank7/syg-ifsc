If your branch is not here

Punjab National Bank has 13,048 branches with an IFSC code, and only 128 of them carry a SWIFT code of their own in our data. For any other branch, ask the bank which SWIFT code to give to the sender. See how to receive money from abroad.

Questions

What is the SWIFT code of Punjab National Bank?

Punjab National Bank codes begin with PUNBIN. In our list, 128 of its 13,048 branches have a code of their own; for example Dhuri-Main, Near Railway Phatak is PUNBINBBABC. For a branch that is not in the list, ask the bank which code to use.

My Punjab National Bank branch is not in this list. What do I give the sender?

Ask your branch. Banks route money for branches without a SWIFT code through a main or foreign-exchange branch, and they tell you which code to use together with your account number and IFSC code.

Can I use the SWIFT code of another Punjab National Bank branch?

Only if the bank tells you to. The money is matched to your account by the account number, but a code the bank did not give you can delay the payment or send it back.
